Habit Chain Support

Habit Chain helps you build linked routines, record honest effort, and understand your consistency.

Quick help

  • Create a routine with a reliable starting anchor.
  • Add habits in the order you want to perform them.
  • Choose Minimum, Target, Strong, or Skip for today.
  • Use Insights to review 7-day and 30-day consistency.

Data and accounts

Habit Chain requires no account and stores its information locally. Deleting the app removes that local information, subject to device backups.
