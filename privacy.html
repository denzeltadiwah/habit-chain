Habit Chain Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Your information stays on your device

Habit Chain works without an online account. The name you optionally provide, your routines, habits, settings, and completion history are stored locally on your device.

No collection or tracking

We do not receive, collect, sell, rent, or share this information. Habit Chain does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Deleting your information

Deleting Habit Chain removes its locally stored information, subject to your device and backup settings. You can also remove individual routines and habits inside the app.

External links

The app may open a public James Clear webpage when you choose an external learning resource. Your use of that website is governed by its own privacy practices. Habit Chain is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by James Clear.

Future changes

If a future version adds cloud synchronization, accounts, analytics, or other data collection, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those features are released.
